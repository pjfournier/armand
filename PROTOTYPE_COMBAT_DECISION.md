# DECIDED — Prototype Combat Direction

The Callum house prototype is authored as a stealth, evasion, and observation encounter rather than a conventional combat encounter. Full combat resolution is deferred beyond the prototype unless playtesting demonstrates that it is necessary to the game's core experience.

The returning cultists provide movement, uncertainty, sound cues, hiding, avoidance, observation, eavesdropping, deception, distraction, Guillermo scouting/distraction, Darkness, Misty Step, escape, timing pressure, and environmental interaction. Prototype success must be possible through investigation, stealth, social play, magic, Guillermo, and evasion. Phase 6 therefore does not depend on a complete 5e combat loop, separate combat mode, or combat-specific Notebook architecture.

This is a prototype scope decision, not a declaration that the final game will never contain combat. Armand's HP, AC, combat spells, and damage state, plus Guillermo's mortality, remain valid data and are not removed.

Until a future combat design phase, deliberate violence may use an already-supported Phase 3 resolution only where that mechanic represents the action honestly. Unsupported combat sequences must be rejected or deferred explicitly. The prototype must never imply that a hidden or comprehensive combat engine exists.
